1:08 Rote Learning and Meaningful

Learning

Rote learning is reproduction of the learned
material verbatim. The learner is not interested
in checking whether the material learned by him
is correct or incorrect. On the other hand in
meaningful learning, the learners understand
and internalize the matter they learn. So they
do not try to reproduce the matter learned as
such but try to express it in their own language.
In other words, the output of the meaningful
learner will be much more than the input they

1:09:2 Definition of ‘Active Learning’

Active learning is “anything that involves
students in doing things and thinking about the
things they are doing” (Bonwell & Eison, 1991).

According to Weltman ‘Active Learning is a
method of learning in which students are actively
or experientially involved in the learning process.
There are different levels of active learning,
depending on student-involvement’.

Felder & Brent (2009) define active learning
as “anything course-related that all students in
a class session are called upon to do other than
simply watching, listening and taking notes”

In brief, Active Learning includes two aspects
— 1) students doing some' learning activities,
ii) thinking about the things they are doing.
