Plato was born in Athens ot the ancient country
Greece between 424 and 423 B.C in an aristocratic
family. In his youth, Plato showed interest in poetry
and being a poet, later when he met Socrates, he
burnt his poems and turned to philosophy. Socrates
methods of dialogue and debate impressed Plato
so much that soon he became his close associate
and dedicated his life to the question of virtue and
the formation of a noble character. Like Socrates,
Plato too used dialogue and debate in carrying out
philosophical enquiries in his attempt to find the

epistemological truth.

Sense perception does not reveal the true
reality of things,. but gives us mere appearance.
Opinions developed from perceptions may be true
or false. According to Plato, one has to enquire by
raising questions like ‘Why’, ‘How’ ‘What is the
reason for it?’ etc. and subject the obtained data

According to him, power of sight is in the eyes and

can observe everything correctly. Similarly all the
knowledge is already there in the learners’ mind
deeply hidden; bringing them out by making the
child to discover by himself is all what we mean by
teaching. Putting out suitable questions to make
students think and answer, asking further questions

is what true teaching is.

Questioning, discussion, logical arguments,

Social interaction strategies begin with an
introduction of the topic/objectives of the lesson

respective teams formed already. in a
predetermined method, each consisting of 5 or 6
students. Team members discuss among
themselves the concepts in the given topic. In such
discussions students in pairs or in small sub-
groups with 2 or 3 members, can actively engage
themselves and discuss the topic. If any of the
concepts in the topic could not be understood by
the members of a sub-group, then they can consult
the members of the other sub-groups in the team.

bese of Teaching 207
|

The teacher should continue to monitor the teams
so that they stay on task and engage themselves
actively in discussing and sharing information
among themselves. In each team some members
will serve as facilitators and others as learners. At
the end of the allotted time, the representative of
each team {mostly the one who served as facilitator
in the group) will present the reviewed report of
his team to other teams. In this way all the students
in the class learn all the concepts and facts
contained in the topic. The teacher should ensure
during the whole process that interactions are
student to student. Students must be active and
responsible for their learning, both acting as a
facilitator and learner. Students will be able to
explain the content of the topic to one another as
well as the entire class. The team working skills of
compromise, negotiation and motivation get
developed and used in the learning process.
