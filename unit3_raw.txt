and integrates it with his ‘existing cognitive

3:05 -Nature of Learning Process as per
the Constructivist Theory

According to the constructivist theory,
construction of knowledge takes place in short term
memory. (This is also known as working memory
of the learners). In this process, the learner uses
the new information obtained from his environment
and the knowledge already stored in his long term
memory i.e. to meaningfully interpret the new
information received; he selects the appropriate
ones from the knowledge in store of his memory
and compares with them. Then he organizes the
new information and integrates them with his
knowledge pool. :

There are three important activities in the
process of learning. They are: | i

i) Selecting the most appropriate from among
the information received: selecting appropriate

in memory in the form of audio and video

‘selecting’ and ‘meaningfully interpreting’
take place.

Classifying and organizing the new

information (coded in the form of audio and

Integrating the new information which has
been well organized (i.e. new knowledge)

UNIT - IV
MODELS OF TEACHING

4:00 Introduction

According to the instructional objectives,
teacher has to set his teaching approaches and
techniques. For example if ‘knowing’ is the primary
objective of learning then ‘memory level teaching’
advocated by Herbart is the best suited one. On
the other hand if pupil's ‘understanding’ is taken
up as the instructional objective, then Morrison’s
approach is to be followed in teaching. After
finalising the objectives of teaching, then the
appropriate teaching - learning situations and
classroom interactions needed for achieving the
teaching objectives are to be specified along with
the ways and means of realising them. A model of
teaching is precisely attempting to explain these
things.
