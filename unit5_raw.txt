5:04:5 Having Reflective Practices

As new technologies get introduced in the
society every day and subject knowledge is
expanding rapidly, it is essential for the professional
teacher not to get satisfied himself with his pre-
service training and classroom teaching
experiences but he should reflect on his own

The educational and professional qualifications

well defined. Firstly, only those who possess these
